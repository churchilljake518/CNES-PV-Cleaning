Notes:
- Data is organized by inverter number. There is both 2024 and 2025 data for inverters 9,10, and 14.
- Be aware there might be gaps between the different csv files. A particularly noticeable one is between 2024 and 2025 when data was not recorded for several weeks. 
- Also note some of the files already have the Energy calculated from the current readinigs (like Inv 9 2-16-25) and some do not (like Inv 9 2-2-25).
- The dataset have different time steps of 20, 30 or 60 seconds. I believe all data 8-25-24 and later has one minute steps, the 7-18-24 data is 30 seconds, and everything bfore that is 20 second.
- Inverter 14 experienced significant issues and was nonfunctional for an extended period. So there is a good bit of missing data there.
- I don't remember when exactly the cleanings occurred, but I could go back and check. I believe the first cleaning for array 10 and late Feb 2024. Could look into this more at some point...
- The parquet file is for the data viewer tool in Github
